TerraFill — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026 · Applies to the TerraFill Android app (com.yanidv.terrafill)

In short: TerraFill does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no internet access at all. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app requests no Android permissions of any kind and declares no network access, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way for us to identify a player.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so you can pick up where you left off. This includes:

  • Which levels you have unlocked, and your best score and star rating for each
  • Stars you have earned and spent
  • Skins you own and the one you have equipped
  • An interrupted game, so it can be resumed
  • Your sound on/off preference

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

Android backup

If you have Android's system backup switched on, your device may copy the game's save data to your own Google account, in the same way it backs up your other apps. That copy belongs to you and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The developer of TerraFill has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's system settings.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party libraries that collect data. Nothing tracks you across apps, and no advertising identifier is read or used.

If this ever changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the Data Safety section on Google Play will be updated to match.

Children

TerraFill is suitable for a general audience. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. No personal information is knowingly or unknowingly gathered from users of any age.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and nothing stored on any server. To erase everything the game remembers:

  • Open the game, go to Options and choose Reset progress, or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its local data

If you also want to remove the system backup copy described above, clear it from your Google account's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with a new effective date at the top. Material changes will be reflected on the app's Google Play listing as well.
